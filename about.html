About CampusVault

Making academic resource sharing easier for Bennett University students.

What is CampusVault?

CampusVault is a student-focused academic resource-sharing platform designed for Bennett University students.

It provides a centralized space where students can access and share academic notes, previous year question papers and other study materials.

Our aim is to make useful academic resources easier to discover and encourage students to learn together.

Our Mission

To create a collaborative learning environment where students can share knowledge and support one another.

What We Offer

Easy Access

Find academic materials in one organized platform.

Resource Sharing

Contribute notes and study materials for others.

Organized Learning

Explore resources by semester and resource type.

Our Team

CampusVault is developed as a Web Development semester project by students of Bennett University.
